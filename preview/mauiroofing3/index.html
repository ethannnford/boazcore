About Us

Maui S.S. Roofing is a locally owned roofing and construction company serving homeowners and businesses across Maui. We install standing seam metal roofing built for salt air, trade winds, and island sun — and because we're licensed for general work, the same crew can handle the framing, decking, and repairs underneath.

We show up when we say we will, we photograph what we find, and we tell you straight whether you need a repair or a roof. Most of our work comes from neighbors telling neighbors, and we'd like to keep it that way.
