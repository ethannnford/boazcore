How we work

A process with no gray areas.

Before we start, you'll have a detailed written scope and a fixed project price broken down by phase. If something changes, it's documented in a formal change order before any work proceeds.

01

Initial consultation

We meet to understand your vision, assess your space, and set the project scope. You'll get a clear walkthrough of the construction process, the timeline, and what to expect at every stage.

02

Permitting & coordination

All permits handled, coordinated with property management, HOAs, and city requirements. From commercial projects to residential remodels, approvals are secured before work begins.

03

Construction, documented daily

Milestone photo updates keep you informed at every stage — you never wonder about progress, you see it. Direct communication throughout, with quality oversight on every trade.

04

Final inspection

A comprehensive walkthrough and punch-list review before completion. Every detail inspected against our standard — and yours.
