Raised around the work.

I grew up on Maui and started on job sites in grade school, working next to my father. I'm the third generation of carpenters in my family.

I studied architecture at the University of Hawaiʻi at Mānoa, spent years as a carpenter for other contractors, and settled in San Diego in 2007. In 2018 I started Travis Ford Woodworks.

I still build with my own two hands, and I care as much about how a project is run as how it is finished. Every job is priced on its scope of work, and my aim on every one is to deliver more than was expected.

"I approach my business as relationship building and each job is about the person." Travis Ford, in SDVoyager's Hidden Gems, 2022
