    About

    Raised aroundthe work.

    I grew up on Maui and started on job sites in grade school, working next to my father. I'm the third generation of carpenters in my family.

    I studied architecture at the University of Hawaiʻi at Mānoa, carpentered for other contractors for years, and settled in San Diego in 2007. In 2018 I started Travis Ford Woodworks.

    Every job is priced on its scope of work, and I care as much about how a project is run as how it's finished.
