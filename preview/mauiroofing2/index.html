Why metal, why here

The island eats
ordinary roofs

Salt, wind, and UV do things to a Maui roof that a mainland shingle was never built for. Here's what you're actually up against — and why we install what we install.

🌊

Salt air

Salt never stops working. It rusts fasteners, eats galvanized flashing, and blisters asphalt from underneath. We spec aluminum and stainless fasteners so the roof isn't corroding the day it goes on.

💨

Trade winds

Shingles lift at the tab and peel in a line. A mechanically-seamed metal panel has no exposed edge to catch wind — it's locked, ridge to eave, with clips that let it move instead of tear.

☀️

Island sun

UV cooks the oils out of asphalt until it goes brittle and cracks. Painted aluminum reflects it, runs cooler over the living space, and holds its finish for decades instead of years.
