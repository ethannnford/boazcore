Our approach

What cognitive rehabilitation actually is

A combination of restorative and compensatory techniques that improve attention, memory, organization, problem-solving, planning and verbal expression.

✓ Evidence-based strategies and exercises, delivered by speech pathologists who specialize in cognition
✓ Built around your real goals — your job, your classes, your life
✓ A clear plan with measurable progress, not an open-ended commitment
